**Supplementary Figure S3. Resident-Level Variation in Autonomy and Performance Ratings**

Caterpillar plots of resident-specific random intercepts (conditional modes, log-odds scale) with 95% intervals from the mixed-effects ordinal models of Table 4 for (A) Zwisch autonomy and (B) SIMPL performance (171 attending evaluations, 35 residents). Values above 0 indicate residents rated higher than expected for their postgraduate year, case complexity, procedure, approach, attending, and timing; residents are ordered by their estimate and colored by postgraduate year.
